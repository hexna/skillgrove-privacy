SkillGrove · 卡片大师 — Privacy Policy

Last updated / 最后更新:2026-09-17

English中文

English

SkillGrove ("the App", also listed as 卡片大师) is a local-first learning app for flashcards, skill trees, reading and AI-assisted study. We do not run an account system and we do not operate a server that stores your study data.

1. Data stored on your device (not uploaded automatically)

Flashcard decks and cards, FSRS review history and statistics, skill trees and question banks, books you import and your reading progress, highlights and notes, book-distillation results, AI chat history, pomodoro/task records, and app settings. All of it lives in the app's private storage. The developer cannot access it. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

2. Features that use the network (triggered only by you)

  1. AI features — chat, translation, card generation, book distillation, skill-tree generation. The text you submit is sent over HTTPS to the provider you selected in Settings (DeepSeek, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, or a third-party gateway you configure yourself), and is processed under that provider's privacy policy.
  2. AI image recognition — when you select a PDF region and choose AI recognition, the selected image is uploaded to the same AI provider.
  3. Dictionary lookup — the looked-up word is sent to free dictionary sources (Free Dictionary API, Wiktionary, UAPI). You can change sources and priority in Settings.
  4. Built-in browser and online reading — the sites you visit (including search engines such as DuckDuckGo, Bing, Google and Baidu) handle your requests under their own policies.
  5. Card images — images are downloaded from image sources when you use that feature.
  6. Model list — available models are fetched from the AI endpoint you configured.

Third-party policies: DeepSeek · OpenAI · Google · OpenRouter · Free Dictionary API

3. Permissions

  • Network (INTERNET) — the online features above.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — only for the pomodoro reminders you start; you can revoke them in system settings.
  • Microphone / speech recognition (only if your build offers dictation) — requested only when you start dictation, to call the system speech service. Audio may be processed by the system speech provider under its policy; the App itself does not store recordings.

None of these permissions are used for advertising or behavioural tracking.

4. Image OCR

Image OCR runs on-device with Tesseract; the image never leaves your device. Only when you explicitly select a PDF region for AI recognition is that image sent to your AI provider.

5. Ads and purchases

The Google Play build shows in-app banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure those ads, AdMob collects your advertising ID (GAID), device and network information, ad impressions and clicks, and a coarse location inferred from your IP address. That data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy; the developer never receives or uploads it. This app does not request personalized ads, so the ads you see are not based on cross-app behavioural profiling. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings or at adssettings.google.com — see policies.google.com/technologies/ads for details.

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent to the use of data for advertising through Google's certified consent management platform (UMP) once the interface has loaded. You can accept or refuse, and change your choice at any time in Me → Privacy policy → Ad privacy options. Refusing does not affect flashcards, review, reading or any other feature — it only means no ads are shown.

The Google Play build also offers a "remove ads" subscription and a one-time lifetime "remove ads" purchase; once active, no ads are shown in the app. Purchases are handled by Google Play and we never see your payment card details.

Other distribution channels (such as the China build) do not integrate any ad network and show no ads.

6. Sharing

Other than the requests you trigger yourself (Section 2), we do not sell, rent or share your personal information. The developer holds no copy of your data. Your AI API key is stored on your device and sent only to the provider you choose — do not save someone else's key on a shared device.

7. Your rights and deletion

  • Delete decks, cards, books, highlights and notes inside the App at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all local data; no developer-side deletion is needed.
  • Content already sent to an AI provider is subject to that provider's policy — contact them to exercise access or deletion rights there.
  • Clear your API key in Settings → AI to stop all AI requests immediately.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. AI-generated content

AI output (translations, cards, summaries, distillations, skill trees, chat replies) may be inaccurate. It is marked with an "AI" badge in the App. Judge before you rely on it and do not treat it as medical, legal or financial advice.

10. Changes

Material changes to data handling will be reflected on this page with a new date.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: shenmibaipiaonan@gmail.com

中文

SkillGrove(中文名:卡片大师)是一款本地优先的学习应用,涵盖闪卡、技能树、阅读与 AI 辅助学习。本应用没有账号系统,开发者也没有存储你学习数据的服务器。

一、保存在你设备上的数据(不会自动上传)

卡片与卡组、FSRS 复习记录与学习统计、技能树与题库、你导入的书籍文件与阅读进度、高亮与笔记、AI 拆书结果、AI 对话历史、番茄钟与任务记录,以及应用设置。这些数据保存在应用私有目录内,开发者无法访问;卸载应用即全部删除。

二、需要联网的功能(只在你主动使用时触发)

  1. AI 功能——对话、翻译、制卡、拆书、技能树生成。你提交的文本会通过 HTTPS 发送到你在设置中选择的服务商(DeepSeek / OpenAI / Gemini / OpenRouter,或你自行填写的第三方中转站),由该服务商按其隐私政策处理。
  2. AI 识图——在 PDF 中框选并选择 AI 识别时,所选区域的图片会一并上传给该 AI 服务商。
  3. 划词词典——查询的单词会发送给免费词典数据源(Free Dictionary API、Wiktionary、UAPI 英汉词典),可在设置中调整数据源与优先级。
  4. 内置浏览器与在线阅读——你访问的网页(包括 DuckDuckGo、Bing、Google、Baidu 等搜索引擎)由对应网站处理你的请求。
  5. 卡片配图——按你的操作从图片来源站点下载图片。
  6. 模型列表——从你配置的 AI 服务地址获取可用模型。

三、设备权限

  • 网络(INTERNET)——上述联网功能。
  • 通知(POST_NOTIFICATIONS)与精确闹钟(SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)——仅用于你开启的番茄钟提醒,可随时在系统中关闭。
  • 麦克风 / 语音识别(仅当你的版本提供语音听写)——只在你主动使用听写时申请,用于调用系统语音识别服务;音频可能由系统语音服务提供商按其政策处理,应用自身不保存录音。

这些权限都不会用于广告或行为追踪。

四、图片文字识别(OCR)

图片 OCR 使用设备本地的 Tesseract 引擎完成,图片不会离开你的设备。只有在 PDF 中主动框选并选择 AI 识别时,该区域图片才会发送给你配置的 AI 服务商。

五、广告与内购

Google Play 版本会展示由 Google AdMob 提供的应用内横幅与插屏广告,用于支撑应用的持续开发。为投放与计量广告,AdMob 会收集你的广告标识符(GAID)、设备与网络信息、广告展示与点击记录,以及按 IP 推断的粗略位置;这些数据由 Google 按其隐私政策处理,开发者本人不会拿到、也不会上传这些数据。本应用不请求个性化广告,投放到你设备上的广告不基于跨应用的行为画像;你仍可在系统设置或 adssettings.google.com 中重置或删除广告 ID。广告相关说明见 policies.google.com/technologies/ads 。

在欧洲经济区(EEA)、英国与瑞士,应用会在界面加载完成后通过 Google 认证的同意管理平台(UMP)征求你对广告数据使用的同意,你可以选择同意或拒绝;之后可随时在「我的 → 隐私政策 → 广告隐私选项」中更改。拒绝同意不影响卡片、复习、阅读等任何其他功能,只是不再展示广告。

Google Play 版本同时提供「订阅去广告」与「买断终身去广告」两种内购:购买生效后应用内不再展示任何广告。交易全部由 Google Play 结算,我们不接触你的支付卡信息。订阅可在 Google Play 中随时取消。

其他发行线(如中国区版本)不接入任何广告网络,不展示广告。

六、数据共享

除上述由你主动触发的请求(第二节)外,我们不会向第三方出售、出租或共享你的个人信息;开发者没有你的数据副本。AI 密钥仅保存在你的设备上,并只发送给你选择的服务商。请勿在公共或共享设备上保存他人的密钥。

七、你的权利与数据删除

  • 可在应用内随时删除卡组、卡片、书籍、高亮与笔记。
  • 卸载应用会清除全部本地数据,开发者无需、也无法代为删除。
  • 已发送给 AI 服务商的内容,请按对应服务商的隐私政策行使查阅与删除权。
  • 可在设置 → AI 中清除 API 密钥,立即停止一切 AI 请求。

八、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童,也不会在明知的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。

九、AI 生成内容

AI 生成结果(翻译、卡片、摘要、拆书、技能树、对话回复)可能不准确,界面会以「AI」标记提示;请自行判断是否采用,不要将其作为医疗、法律或金融等专业建议。

十、政策更新

当数据处理方式发生重大变化时,我们会更新本页面内容与日期。
